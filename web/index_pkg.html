Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Install sloopDX

The FM-1 as a six-operator DX7: voice editing on the device, 256 voices from your own .syx banks, four FM drum kits, and SLOOP's live workflow.

Install about one minute

  2. Press Install and choose the FM-1's MIDI port when the browser asks (allow SysEx).

Use at your own risk. Unofficial custom firmware, free and without any warranty. Back up your projects and banks first; do not unplug while it writes. No liability for damage or data loss. Disclaimer
