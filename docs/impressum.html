sloopDX is unofficial, custom firmware. Installing it replaces the firmware of your M-VAVE FM-1. It is provided free of charge, “as is”, without any warranty, under the GNU GPL 3.0. You install and use it entirely at your own risk. The author accepts no liability for damage to your device, lost data, lost projects or any other loss, as far as the law allows.

An interrupted update can leave the FM-1 unable to start; recovering it may need extra hardware (FM-1-transporter). Back up your projects and banks before you update. Going back to the official firmware is possible with M-VAVE's updater.
