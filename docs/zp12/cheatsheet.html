Each tap of EDIT, FX or SEQ: the next of its pages.

Make a beat

  1. REC (it blinks: armed), PLAY: a bar of count-in.
  2. Play kick and snare. AUTO CORRECT puts them on the grid.
  3. REC again at the end: an empty loop (BARS AUTO) is now as long as your take.
  4. Hats step by step: hit the hat, hold SEQ: the white keys are its 16 steps (lit = a hit), tap to set / clear; OCT+ the next bar.
  5. REC while it plays: overdub more on top; REC again: off.
  6. A chord: OCT+, white 9 (D1). Melodic: ARP (MULTI PITCH).
  7. Another loop: a black key, record into it. Songs: SEQ → SONG.

Buttons

PLAYrun / stop (lit while it runs)
RECstopped: armed (blinks); playing: overdub on / off
LFO + paderase: that pad's hits go as the playhead passes (stopped: all at once)
ENVtap tempo (it blinks on the beat)
SAVEsave now; stopped and quiet for 3 s it saves by itself (sounds, mix, effects, loops, songs)
HOMEthe faders

Effects in 3 steps

  1. Hit the pad (the snare).
  2. EDIT until the LCD says SENDS.
  3. KNOB 4 = REV, KNOB 3 = DLY. Shape them: FX.
